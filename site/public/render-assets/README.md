# Fishy browser render assets

`models/` is generated by the canonical Blender pipeline:

- [`blender/build_natural_assets.py`](../../../blender/build_natural_assets.py)
  exports nine original natural variants across eight unique geometric
  families: `rock-rounded`, `rock-strata`, `wood-arch`, `wood-root`,
  `wood-stump`, `plant-broadleaf`, `plant-grass`, `plant-stem`, and
  `plant-moss`; it also exports scan-derived `plant-fern` from an adapted Poly
  Haven Fern 02 clump b source. The two rock exports share the original
  fractured geometry and vary only in their documented CC0 PBR materials.
- [`blender/build_tetra.py`](../../../blender/build_tetra.py) exports
  `neon-tetra.glb`, including its authored skin, armature, and cyclic swim
  animation.

The current delivery has 11 assets: nine original natural exports across eight
unique geometric families, one adapted CC0 fern, and one original tetra. Its
native sources are
[`fishy-fidelity-assets.blend`](../../../blender/fishy-fidelity-assets.blend)
and [`fishy-neon-tetra.blend`](../../../blender/fishy-neon-tetra.blend). The
separate Ravine riverbank study has 56 editable objects; the scene editor limit
is 64. The legacy browser-to-recipe export remains deliberately limited to 24
approximate procedural envelopes, so it is not a native reproduction path for
that full study.

Astra completed native and browser visual review. The [quality report](../../../workstreams/fidelity-reference-20260921/astra-refinement/QUALITY_REPORT.md) records findings and remaining differences from the supplied ravine reference; exact parity is not claimed. The evidence boundary and recorded method are in
[`astra-refinement/METHOD.md`](../../../workstreams/fidelity-reference-20260921/astra-refinement/METHOD.md).

The eight unique original natural geometric families, their foliage maps, tetra
mesh, tetra skin, and tetra rig are authored work from those generators. The
rock and bark surface maps used by the natural generator are selected Poly
Haven scans under CC0 1.0. `rock-strata` uses the documented `lichen_rock`
scan as a mineral variation of `rock-rounded`'s original fractured geometry.
The exact assets, source URLs, licenses, dimensions, upstream MD5s, and local
SHA-256 hashes are in
[`blender/material-sources/materials-provenance.json`](../../../blender/material-sources/materials-provenance.json).
The active rock/bark selections are in
[`blender/material-sources/selection.json`](../../../blender/material-sources/selection.json).

`plant-fern` uses the adapted Poly Haven Fern 02 clump b source. Poly Haven
credits Rico Cilliers for modeling and Rob Tuytel for scanning; the separate
CC0 source package, unchanged glTF texture references, URLs, MD5s, and
SHA-256s are recorded in
[`blender/material-sources/fern_02/provenance.json`](../../../blender/material-sources/fern_02/provenance.json).
The retained `make_procedural_fern()` is an authoring fallback/reference and is
not the delivered fern geometry.

The editable sources are
[`blender/fishy-fidelity-assets.blend`](../../../blender/fishy-fidelity-assets.blend)
for the nine original natural exports (eight unique geometries) plus the
adapted Fern 02 family, and
[`blender/fishy-neon-tetra.blend`](../../../blender/fishy-neon-tetra.blend) for
the tetra. The exported GLBs embed their material textures. Current generator
outputs in `textures/` include the authored `botanical-leaf-*` and
`tetra-skin-*` maps; older `fishy-*` PNGs remain as legacy files and are not
provenance for this pipeline.

The delivery inspection workflow is
[`blender/inspect_natural_assets.py`](../../../blender/inspect_natural_assets.py),
which imports the GLBs and renders fixed front and reverse Cycles views. It is
an inspection aid; this directory does not assert visual parity or final visual
QA.

The regenerated riverbank study uses Astra-reviewed placements from
[`study-plant-contacts.ts`](../../lib/study-plant-contacts.ts). Its contact
proxy is generated from the delivered stone by
[`blender/export_contact_surface.py`](../../../blender/export_contact_surface.py).
[`blender/seat_study_plants.py`](../../../blender/seat_study_plants.py) writes
candidate contact anchors for review; rerunning it does not automatically
accept them into the study. The exact native reproduction commands are in the
[Blender README](../../../blender/README.md#study-contact-reproduction).

`blender/build_fidelity_assets.py` is an older procedural exporter retained for
historical compatibility. It is not the canonical generator for these assets.
